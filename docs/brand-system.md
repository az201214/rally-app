# Rally Brand System

## Position

Rally is the control system for a player's padel life: precise enough for match
decisions, welcoming enough to build a local playing community. The brand is
confident and premium, never loud, exclusive, or game-like.

## Visual language

Carbon black, midnight navy, graphite, and warm off-white establish the product.
Electric green is a signal—not a background. Court boundaries, service lines,
ball trajectories, glass-panel geometry, and compact telemetry are the visual
vocabulary. Random blobs, crypto neon, ornamental gradients, and unrelated sport
imagery are outside the system.

The Rally mark combines a rounded padel court, center/service lines, and a ball.
It should retain clear space equal to half the mark width and appear on quiet
surfaces. The wordmark uses strong tracking and never stretches or skews.

## Voice

Copy is concise, direct, and useful. Rally says “Find a match,” “Match ready,”
and “Open match chat.” It avoids hype, artificial urgency, fake technical
language, and developer-facing terms in production messages.

## Motion

Motion communicates state and spatial continuity. Micro-interactions run for
150–250 ms; screen reveals normally complete within 400 ms. Use fade, short
translation, subtle scale, count-up, and ball trajectory. Animate opacity and
transform rather than layout. Continuous decorative motion is prohibited.

When reduced motion is enabled, content appears immediately or uses a brief fade.
Animation never blocks input, and a user's action always interrupts presentation.

## Product rules

- One primary action per decision surface.
- Green marks action, selection, success, or live status.
- Athlete identity and match context take priority over decoration.
- Padel-specific geometry must remain subtle enough not to reduce legibility.
- Every screen uses the same spacing, typography, icon, surface, and motion rules.
